LIVING CUTTING

Dragon fruit bypasses true seed cultivation. Growers plant mature, 30–40 cm 3-winged stem cuttings (cladodes) cured in shade to form a suberized callus before planting into fertile, sandy-loam furrows.

04 // Cold Chain Transit

RACE AGAINST TIME
